Privacy Policy for SoulPure

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

SoulPure (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides motivational content. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency and user privacy. By using SoulPure, you agree to the terms described herein.

1. Information Collection and Use

SoulPure is designed with a Privacy-First, Local-Only architecture. We do not maintain external servers to store user profiles or favorites.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII). No account registration is required.
  • Device Permissions: We request POST_NOTIFICATIONS strictly to deliver scheduled inspirational quotes. This is optional.
  • Local Storage: Your "Favorite" quotes and UI settings are stored locally on your device. This data is never uploaded to any cloud service.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show advertisements. These services may use advertising IDs to serve relevant ads.

  • AdMob Policy: For more info, visit Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Children’s Privacy

SoulPure is a general audience app with family-friendly content. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us immediately.

4. Data Security & Deletion Control

Because your data is stored locally, you have full control over your information at all times.

  • In-App Reset: You can permanently delete all favorites and saved settings by going to Settings > Reset App Data directly within the SoulPure application.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app automatically removes all locally stored data associated with SoulPure from your device.
  • No Data Retention: We do not keep copies or backups of your interaction history once you delete the app or reset your data.
