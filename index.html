Privacy Policy for SoulPure

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

SoulPure (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides motivational content and utility features. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency and user privacy. By using SoulPure, you agree to the terms described herein.

1. Information Collection and Use

SoulPure is designed with a Privacy-First, Local-Only architecture. We do not maintain external servers to store user profiles.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses. No account registration is required to use the app.
  • Device Permissions: We request the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission only to deliver scheduled inspirational quotes. This is optional and can be disabled in system settings.
  • Local Storage: Your "Favorite" quotes and UI theme settings are stored locally on your device using SQLite/Room database. This data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep SoulPure free, we use Google AdMob to show advertisements. These third-party services may use technology like cookies or advertising IDs to collect non-sensitive information about your device.

  • Google AdMob: Collects anonymous data (like device model and ad interactions) to serve relevant ads. You can reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.
  • Policy Link: For more info, visit Google’s Privacy & Terms and How Google uses data.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SoulPure is a general audience app providing family-friendly motivational content. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately, and we will verify and ensure such data is not being used by our third-party ad partners.

4. Data Security, Deletion & Reset

Because your data is stored locally, you have full control over your information.

  • Manual Reset: You can reset all app data by going to Settings > Apps > SoulPure > Storage > Clear Data. This action permanently deletes your favorites and settings from the device.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app automatically removes all locally stored data associated with SoulPure.
  • No Cloud Backups: We do not keep copies of your "Favorites" or interaction history on any cloud service.
