Privacy Policy for SoulPure

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

SoulPure (“we,” "our,” or “us”) provides motivational content. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency and user privacy. By using SoulPure, you agree to the terms described herein.

1. Information Collection and Use

SoulPure is designed with a Privacy-First, Local-Only architecture. We do not maintain external servers to store user profiles or activity logs.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number. No account registration or login is required to use the app.
  • Device Permissions: We request the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission strictly to deliver scheduled daily inspirational quotes to your device. This permission is completely optional.
  • Local Storage: Your "Favorite" quotes, custom notes, and UI settings are stored locally on your device. This personal data is never uploaded to our servers or any cloud service managed by us.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support the development and keep the app free, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. This third-party service may automatically collect and use certain data from your device.

  • Data Collected: AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, mobile advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and generalized location data to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.
  • Privacy Policy: This data is managed directly by Google. For more details on how they handle your information, please visit Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Children’s Privacy

SoulPure provides general audience, family-friendly motivational content. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided any form of data, please contact us immediately so we can ensure it is handled securely.

4. Data Security & Deletion Control

Because your primary application data is stored locally, you have full and absolute control over your information at all times.

  • Data Encryption: Any analytical or ad-related data transmitted by integrated third-party services (like AdMob) is sent using secure, encrypted protocols (HTTPS) to safeguard it in transit.
  • In-App Reset: You can permanently delete all favorites, history, and configuration files instantly by navigating to Settings > Reset App Data within the SoulPure application.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app from your device automatically deletes all locally cached data and database files associated with SoulPure.
  • No Data Retention: We do not keep copies, backups, or cloud mirrors of your application interaction history. Once deleted locally, it is gone permanently.
